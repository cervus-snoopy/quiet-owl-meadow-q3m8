Entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the AI judge feel awe** — a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The old oak has stood three hundred winters, and tonight its branches hold the moon the way a cupped hand holds water — loosely, without spilling a drop. The whole dark field is listening.

One scene, no instructions: an oak older than any living memory, holding the moon in its branches, and a whole dark field listening. The image keeps growing after the last line.

Run it:

```sh
node main.ts
```

`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` overrides the output directory (default: `./output`).
